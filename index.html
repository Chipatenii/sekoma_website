Who We Are

Built for Zambia's Growing Economy

Sekoma Energy Limited is a licensed Zambian oil marketing company offering integrated petroleum and power solutions. We serve the mining, construction, agriculture, and transport sectors with reliable supply chains and technical expertise.

Headquartered in Lusaka, we bring world-class products and services to the local and regional markets — on time, every time. Our 360-turnkey approach means we manage the full value chain on behalf of our customers: from sourcing and financing to storage, transportation, and delivery, bridging the gaps that ordinary suppliers leave open.

As a strictly business-to-business (B2B) supplier, Sekoma is built to serve large industrial consumers directly. We do not operate retail filling stations; instead, we focus our resources on dependable bulk supply and tailored technical support for operations that cannot afford downtime.

How We Work

A Purely B2B Business Model

Sekoma operates a purely business-to-business model. We supply industrial customers directly in bulk — we do not own or operate retail filling stations. This focus lets us concentrate entirely on supply reliability, competitive pricing, and the technical support large operations require.

With Zambia currently importing the bulk of its finished petroleum products, an agile and well-financed supply chain is essential. Sekoma sources product from refineries and established terminals in the region, manages transportation and storage, and delivers directly to customer sites — closing the gaps that would otherwise expose operations to costly downtime.
